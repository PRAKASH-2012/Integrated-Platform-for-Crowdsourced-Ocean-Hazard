Addressing Ocean Hazards with AI & Social Analytics

India's 7,500 km coastline faces recurring cyclones, tsunamis, high swells, and oil spills. OceanShield AI bridges citizens, rescue forces, and government officers into a unified real-time intelligence hub.

Crowdsourced Hazard Verification

Citizens upload photos, videos, or voice recordings of hazards. Our AI Computer Vision instantly validates authenticity, filters duplicates, and scores severity.

Social Media Crisis Analytics

Monitors Twitter/X, Facebook, and local news headlines to detect panic sentiment spike, trending emergency hashtags, and emergency distress signals.

GIS Live Map & Safe Route Solver

Interactive Leaflet map displaying active hazard heatmaps, safe shelters, medical units, coast guard stations, and shortest evacuation path routing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does OceanShield AI require a backend server?

No! All data persistence, AI simulations, role permissions, and GIS calculations run entirely client-side using Vanilla JS, LocalStorage, and JSON datasets.

How does the AI Image Analyzer detect hazards?

Our modular JavaScript AI engine simulates computer vision feature matching, returning confidence percentages, damage severity levels, and automated safety recommendations.

Can I test different user roles?

Yes! You can instantly switch between Citizen, Government Officer, Rescue Team, and System Admin roles using the quick role switcher in the top dashboard bar.
